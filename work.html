Dental group · Technical SEO & AI search

Academy Dental

Scaling impressions and rankings at the same time — sustainable growth rather than a traffic spike.

Strategy and execution

  • Built topical authority clusters around core dental services.
  • Implemented programmatic on-page optimisation for service and location pages.
  • Optimised for high-intent long-tail keywords with low competition and high conversion.
  • Improved click-through using intent-driven title and meta testing.
  • Strengthened internal linking with contextual relevance mapping.
  • Fixed indexing inefficiencies and crawl budget wastage.
  • Used Google Search Console data for continuous query expansion.

Organic performance

  • 1.09M impressions.
  • 6.77K clicks.
  • Average position improved from 23 to 10.8.

User and engagement growth (GA4)

  • 43K active users, up 305%.
  • 27K engaged sessions, up 274%.
  • 345K events, up 383%.
  • 15K key events (conversions), up 192%.
  • Organic search became the top channel at 24K sessions, ahead of direct at 10K and paid at 3.4K.

Orthodontics · Local SEO & AI search

Smile Republic Orthodontics

Growing search revenue inside a low-demand, highly specific niche.

The challenge

Limited search demand in a narrow niche within a small-town market restricted organic growth potential.

The solution

  • Expanded targeting beyond the primary location to capture demand from nearby communities.
  • Built geo-targeted landing pages for nearby high-potential areas.
  • Focused on service and location intent keywords.
  • Strengthened internal linking for location relevance signals.
  • Optimised the Google Business Profile for multi-location visibility.

Results

  • 1.26M impressions and 5.37K clicks.
  • Average position improved to 12.8.
  • 16K users, up 1600%, and 20K sessions, up 1100%.
  • 87K events, up 627%.
  • Organic search led acquisition at 7.4K sessions, ahead of direct at 4.7K and paid at 2.4K.
