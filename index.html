01About

Architecture first, tactics after.

Most sites do not have a content problem. They have a structure problem: pages that compete with each other, internal links that lead nowhere useful, and a crawl budget spent on templates nobody searches for.

I spend two years' worth of audits fixing that first, then build keyword and content strategy on top of it. It is slower to start and it compounds far harder.

Three things I do differently

Before a single title tag is touched, I map the crawl path: what gets indexed, what competes, what orphans. Restructuring a site's architecture and internal linking is what produced the 120% organic traffic increase at MediaNV, not new content.

Running 25+ Google Business Profiles across the USA and Canada teaches you which local ranking factors actually move the needle and which are folklore. The same playbook then scales to multi-location clients without diluting relevance.

Generative and answer engines quote sources they can parse. Clean entity markup, answer-shaped headings and unambiguous schema are now ranking infrastructure, so I build GEO and AEO into the page rather than bolting it on later.

04Process

How the work runs.

Six stages, in order. Open any one for detail.

1

A full technical crawl in Screaming Frog, cross-referenced with SEMrush, Ahrefs, Search Console and Analytics. The output is a prioritised list of what is costing the site rankings right now, not a 200-page PDF.

2

Restructure the URL hierarchy, consolidate competing pages, and rebuild internal linking so authority flows to the pages that earn revenue. This is the stage that produced the 120% traffic increase.

3

Map every target query to a page and a stage of intent. High-intent long-tail terms usually convert better than the head term everyone is fighting over, so they get built first.

4

Titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, schema markup and media, written to be both ranked and quoted. Content is structured so an answer engine can lift a correct answer from it.

5

Business listings, citation consistency and white-hat link acquisition from relevant, authoritative sites. 30+ quality backlinks in the first month at NCS GX took Domain Authority from 0 to 3.

6

Search Console and GA4 reporting against the queries that matter, then query expansion from what the data surfaces. SEO that is not measured monthly is just publishing.
