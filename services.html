01Expertise

What I do

Four areas of work. They overlap on purpose — a technical fix that nobody can find in a map pack is only half a result.

Areas of work

01Technical SEO

Make the site crawlable, then make it fast.

Audits in SEMrush, Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, Search Console and Analytics to find crawl issues, indexing problems and performance gaps — then the architecture work that actually fixes them.

  • Full technical audit with a prioritised fix list
  • Website architecture and URL hierarchy restructure
  • Internal linking plan mapped to revenue pages
  • Crawlability, broken links and redirect chain repair
  • Core Web Vitals and mobile usability
  • Schema markup implementation

02On-page & content

Write for the intent, not the volume.

Keyword research and search intent analysis that identifies where visibility converts into leads, then on-page work that gives every target query a page built to answer it.

  • Keyword research and search intent analysis
  • Keyword map: one query cluster, one page
  • Topical authority clusters around core services
  • Title and meta description testing for click-through
  • Heading structure and content optimisation
  • Programmatic on-page for service and location pages

03Local SEO & GBP

Show up in the map pack, in every service area.

25+ Google Business Profiles managed for clients in the USA and Canada, plus the geo-targeted page work that captures demand sitting outside a business's home city.

  • Google Business Profile setup, verification and optimisation
  • Category and service selection strategy
  • Citation building and NAP consistency
  • Geo-targeted landing pages for nearby areas
  • Local pack tracking and review signals
  • Multi-location visibility

04AI search — GEO & AEO

Be the source the answer engine quotes.

Generative Engine Optimization and Answer Engine Optimization: content and markup structured so an AI system can extract a correct, attributable answer and cite the site it came from.

  • Entity and brand clarity across the site
  • Question-shaped headings and answer-first copy
  • Structured data that removes ambiguity
  • Content aligned to current Google and AI search behaviour
  • AI-assisted content workflows and prompt engineering
  • Team training on AI-assisted SEO practice
